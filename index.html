Order Desk

Ritual Matcha2026

Wholesale customers order through Viber. The Order Desk reads those chats and turns them into Shopify draft orders, so nobody re-types them. It also tracks which orders are still waiting for payment.

  • Used by the whole sales team, with orders from 300+ customers.
  • Matches each chat order against 1,000+ Shopify accounts before drafting it.
  • Saves the wholesale team about 2 hours a day.
